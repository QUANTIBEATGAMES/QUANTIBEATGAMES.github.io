BLOCKS ON THE BEAT

A block puzzle you play to the music. Place your pieces, tap on the beat to drop them and stack the chain. 13 tracks across four albums, a calm Relax mode and a Daily Board for everyone.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email quantibeat.games@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

  • Restore purchases: open the Shop (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Album packs and All Access come back on any device signed in to the same Apple or Google account.
  • Hints are stored on your device and are not restored if you delete the app.
  • Drops feel late or early? Open Calibrate from the home screen and follow the taps. Bluetooth headphones usually need a bigger offset.
  • Ad and privacy choices: Settings › Privacy choices.

Privacy
