QUANTIBEAT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Applies to the QUANTIBEAT app on iPhone and Android.

The short version
  • There are no accounts. Your scores, levels, coins, achievements, streaks, settings and boosters are stored on your device. I don't run servers for the game.
  • The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you're asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone you're also asked Apple's tracking permission.
  • If you agree, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics (via PostHog, hosted in the EU) so I can balance the game.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and RevenueCat keeps a record of what you bought so it can be restored.
  • You can change your choices at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy choices.

1. Who I am

QUANTIBEAT is made and published by Scot Coates, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"). I am the controller of the personal data processed through the app under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: quantibeat.games@gmail.com

3. Advertising, consent and tracking

Consent message. When you first open the app in the UK, EEA or Switzerland you see Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, certified for the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). You can accept, reject or manage choices for each purpose and ad partner. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads, which use contextual information and may still use device storage for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement as described in Google's policies.

Apple App Tracking Transparency (iPhone). The app asks Apple's permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using activity from other apps. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (wording varies by device).

Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch one to continue a run, double your coins or get a free booster.

How Google uses data. Google acts as an independent controller for some advertising purposes. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ad partners that may serve ads through AdMob are listed in the consent message's "Vendors" view.

Ad content. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (Google's "PG" maximum ad content rating), and sensitive categories are not used to target ads.

4. Withdrawing consent or changing your choices

Open Settings › Privacy choices in the app at any time to reopen the consent message and change or withdraw your choices, including turning off analytics. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place. On iPhone, tracking permission is controlled in iOS Settings as described above.

5. Who data is shared with

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd: AdMob advertising and consent management. Privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): in-app purchase management, acting as my processor. Privacy policy.
  • PostHog Inc.: product analytics on PostHog EU Cloud (data hosted in Frankfurt, Germany), acting as my processor. Privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and payments, under their own privacy policies.

6. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK, transfers rely on the UK–US Data Bridge (for certified organisations), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or other safeguards permitted by UK law. PostHog analytics data is stored in the EU.

7. How long data is kept

  • On-device game data: until you delete the app or clear its data.
  • PostHog analytics: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • RevenueCat purchase records: for as long as needed to provide and restore purchases and to meet tax and accounting obligations, or until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • Google advertising data: according to Google's retention policy.

8. Children

QUANTIBEAT is a general-audience game. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and it is not in Apple's Kids category or Google Play's Families programme. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app keeps privacy-protective defaults for all players: no accounts, no chat, no precise location, and personalised ads and analytics off unless you consent. If you believe a child has given me personal data, get in touch and it will be deleted.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because there are no accounts, I may need a purchase receipt or transaction ID to find your data. Email quantibeat.games@gmail.com. I reply within one month.

If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

10. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. Unused hints are stored on the device only and are lost if you delete the app. Album packs and All Access can be restored with Restore purchases in the Shop or Settings. On Android, if Google's device backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's on-device data to your Google account. You control this in Android settings, and I cannot access it. To ask for purchase or analytics records held by RevenueCat or PostHog to be deleted, email me.

11. Security

Data sent by the app to these partners is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). There are no game servers of my own.

12. Changes

This page will be updated when the app or the partners it uses change, and the "Last updated" date will change. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
